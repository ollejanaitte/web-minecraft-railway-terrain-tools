4. Final UX Contract
====================================================================

START_WEB_MINECRAFT.sh flow:
  normal runtime/profiles/game -> World -> /railsys3 wand -> two-click placement.

Wand item:
  reuse the existing Railsys Marker Wand in the tools category. No second item.

POS1:
  ordinary right click, store block-centred position + player direction.

POS2:
  ordinary right click while normally facing POS1. Preserve:
  start tangent ~= POS1 forward
  end tangent   ~= -POS2 forward

Arrow:
  world-anchored POS1/POS2 arrows are product UX. Production ownership moves
  out of validation package in R10.

Auto Preview:
  immediately after POS2. Uses the production Anchor/Geometry/RailPath pipeline.
  No renderer-only fake preview and no immediate confirm.

Editing:
  POS1/POS2 direction, curve handle, gradient/pitch, cant, active asset. Every
  line edit rebuilds the same production preview. Asset does not alter line.

Confirm candidate comparison:
  A Shift+right-click: chosen; deliberate, existing hook, touch fallback.
  B third right-click: rejected; accidental confirmation risk.
  C long hold: rejected; browser/touch timing ambiguity.
  D dedicated key: deferred; needs binding/touch UI.
  E /railsys3 confirm: required fallback, not the primary gesture.

Confirm final recommendation:
  Shift + right click is confirm-only. No preview means error/no state change.

Cancel/Clear comparison and recommendation:
  Shift+left-click rejected due block-break/touch conflict.
  Dedicated key deferred.
  /railsys3 cancel: selected; discard preview only, keep markers/edit values.
  /railsys3 clear: selected; clear transient placement session only, keep
  confirmed rail(s) and active asset.
  Confirmed rail deletion is a separate ID-targeted future operation after
  persistent IDs exist; never overloaded onto confirm/cancel/clear.

After Confirm:
  preview-identical production line remains; transient arrows/preview/edit
  visuals clear; current R10 single confirmed slot may be replaced by another
  confirmation until R12 supplies durable multi-rail storage.

  doc/specifications/PHASE1_FINAL_UX_CONTRACT.md

6. Real 3D Model Transition
====================================================================

OPTION A — immediately after R10 without a prior contract freeze:
  NOGO. Early appearance is useful, but contract churn can parallel UX.

OPTION B — after Persistence / Network:
  NOGO. Unstable asset identity/version/origin enters saves and causes migration.

OPTION C — immediately after Asset Contract Freeze and before Persistence:
  GO / FREEZE.

Operational order:
  R10 GO -> R11-A Contract Freeze -> R11-B Real 3D implementation/validation
  -> R12 Persistence.

Technical methods:
  A repeated rigid whole-track segment: rejected as production baseline.
  B full dynamic extrusion for every part: rejected for discrete sleepers.
  C Hybrid: GO / FREEZE.

Hybrid definition:
  - running rail head/web/foot: deformable continuous profile extrusion;
  - sleeper/fastener/accessory: original rigid 3D meshes placed by s[m];
  - original material, PNG texture, UV required;
  - same RailPath supplies straight/curve/gradient/cant;
  - R5 Continuous Rail and R6 Cant remain intact.

  doc/decisions/ADR-012-REAL_3D_RAIL_MODEL_TRANSITION.md
