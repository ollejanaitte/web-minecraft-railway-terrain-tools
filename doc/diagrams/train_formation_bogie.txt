Train / Formation / TrainCar / Bogie relation (ASCII)

TrainFormation
  formationId, direction, speed
  path: RailPath
  leaderPathDistance: double
  cars[0..n-1]  (cars[0] = lead/control)
    |
    +-- TrainCar
    |     def: TrainDefinition (halfLength, bogiePos, size, seats, ...)
    |     frontBogie: Bogie   (path pos at +bogiePos.z)
    |     rearBogie : Bogie   (path pos at -bogiePos.z)
    |     pose: TrainPose {x,y,z,yaw,pitch,roll}  derived from bogies
    |     couplerFront / couplerRear: Coupler (gap = halfLenA+halfLenB)
    |     trainState: door/light/panto/notch/...
    |
    +-- (light car entity for culling/interaction/seats)

Movement per tick (server):
  leader: leaderPathDistance += direction * speed * dt
          path.resolve -> piece/dir/localM -> sample front bogie
          rear bogie at bogieSpan behind front
          body pose = f(frontBogie, rearBogie)   [yaw=chord, pitch=dy,
                                                  roll=avg cant]
  follower k: targetDistance = leaderPathDistance - k*carSpacing
          walk backward across path entries -> piece/dir/localM
          -> front bogie -> rear bogie -> pose

Key fix vs v1: followers never jump to leader's piece; they stay on the
previous piece until their own distance crosses the node.
