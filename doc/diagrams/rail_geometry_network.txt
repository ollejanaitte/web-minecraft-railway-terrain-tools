RailGeometry (pure shape, immutable)
  kind: STRAIGHT | CURVE | VERTICAL_CURVE | SWITCH | TURNTABLE
  start/end + control points + arc table
  sampleByDistance(d) -> RailSample{x,y,z,yaw,pitch,roll,...}
        ^
        |  one geometry per piece (branch: switch has N sub-geometries)
        |
RailPiece (world + state)          RailNode (routing)
  pieceId, defId, corePos            nodeId, x,y,z
  geometry: RailGeometry             connectedPieceIds[]
  worldBlocks[] (rail+ballast)       |
  switchState (SWITCH)               |
  signalRefs[]                       v
        |  start/end node refs  -->  adjacency / route search
        v
RailNetwork
  pieces registry (by id)
  nodes adjacency
  RailRoute (A* plan)  --- route history ---> RailPath entries
  signals / stations / crossings / catenary refs
  SpatialIndex + ActiveRailCache
