# ADR-004: Bogie Representation
Status: Accepted (Phase -1)
## Context
RTM uses bogie entities (two per car) [R-I]. Browser/TeaVM is single-thread
and memory-constrained; 16-car trains with 32 bogie entities each is costly.
## Decision
Bogies are LOGICAL ANCHORS (pure math objects owned by TrainCar), not
physics entities. One lightweight car entity per car for culling/
interaction/seats. Bogie visuals render from anchor poses.
## Alternatives
- RTM-style bogie entities - rejected (entity/tracking/packet cost).
- Hybrid (anchor + render helper) - chosen; render helper is part of car.
## Pros
Low entity count; cheap sync (car pose + state); simple.
## Cons
Loses per-bogie extensibility; acceptable for parity.
## Risks
Seat/occupancy logic must use car/formation, not bogie entities.
## Consequences
Bogie pose = front/rear at bogiePos offsets; body derived from them.
