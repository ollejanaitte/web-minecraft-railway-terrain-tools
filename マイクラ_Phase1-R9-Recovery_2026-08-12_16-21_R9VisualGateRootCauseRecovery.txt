Confirmed root cause
--------------------
RailSegmentDrawer's R3 validation geometry encoded offsets for path Y=5 and
ground top Y=4. R7 marker paths use clicked-surface Y=4. Production therefore
emitted sleepers near Y=3.07 and rails near Y=3.21, inside the grass block,
where normal depth testing hid them. Existing screenshots showed only the
separately elevated marker arrow, exactly matching this failure. This was not
a fog, lighting, texture, blend or framebuffer defect.

Secondary weakness: the R9 contract lacked rail width/height and sleeper
length/height, while sleeperWidthM was not consumed, leaving visible shape
difference dependent on gauge/color alone.

Fix / affected behavior
-----------------------
Added production-only RailPath-relative surface emission and consumed the
look-only profile dimensions. Preserved the legacy R3-R6 drawing methods,
R7/R8 workflow, ModelPack loader/registry/fallback, continuous-span contract,
Geometry/RailPath, curve, gradient, cant and normal Minecraft fog.
